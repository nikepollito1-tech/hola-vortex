Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Summary

"Hola, Vórtex" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hola, Vórtex channel (the "Owner") to publish the channel's own videos on the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and to read the statistics of those accounts. It has no other users and does not collect personal data from the public.

2. Data the App handles

  • Authorisation tokens for the Owner's own accounts: TikTok (through Login Kit), YouTube (through Google OAuth) and Instagram (through Meta), so the App can publish videos on the Owner's behalf.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (such as display name), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Statistics of the Owner's own channel and videos (views, likes, comments, audience retention and subscribers gained), read through the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API (read-only) and the Instagram API, and shown only to the Owner.
  • The videos and captions the Owner produces and uploads.

The App does not access the data of viewers, followers or any other user of these platforms.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the Owner's videos and to show the Owner the statistics of their own accounts. Tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the Owner and are not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party. Videos are sent to each platform through its official API.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the App is connected to each account. The Owner can disconnect the App at any time, either by deleting the tokens or from each platform's own settings (for Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok, "Manage app permissions"; for Instagram, "Apps and websites"), after which access ends and the tokens are deleted.

5. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted by GitHub Pages; both providers may record technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by direct message to @holavortex0 on TikTok.
