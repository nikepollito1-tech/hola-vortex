Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Summary

"Hola, Vórtex" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hola, Vórtex channel (the "Owner") to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It has no other users and does not collect personal data from the public.

2. Data the App handles

  • TikTok authorisation tokens for the Owner's account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit, so the App can upload videos on the Owner's behalf.
  • Basic profile information of the Owner's TikTok account (such as display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions the Owner produces and uploads.

The App does not access the data of viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

3. How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to upload the Owner's videos to TikTok. Tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the Owner and are not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party. Videos are sent to TikTok through its official API.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the App is connected to the TikTok account. The Owner can disconnect the App at any time, either in the App or from the TikTok account settings ("Manage app permissions"), after which the tokens are deleted.

5. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted by GitHub Pages; both providers may record technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by direct message to @holavortex0 on TikTok.
